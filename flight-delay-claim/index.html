Questions

Does my flight qualify?

EU rules cover flights that departed an EU airport (any airline), or arrived in the EU on an EU airline, that arrived 3+ hours late, were cancelled with under 14 days' notice, or overbooked. Bad weather and air-traffic-control strikes usually don't count.

My flight was inside the US.

US law doesn't pay compensation for delays, so we can't claim it. If the flight was cancelled, you're owed a refund from the airline directly.

How much could I get?

€250 for flights up to 1,500 km, €400 up to 3,500 km, €600 beyond that, per passenger.

Do you go to court?

No. We file with the airline and the national enforcement body. If the airline refuses and you want to sue, we'll tell you so and charge nothing.

Do airlines accept claims from you?

Most do with your signed authorization. A few (Ryanair, for one) only deal with passengers directly; for those we prepare the claim and every follow-up, and you send them from your own email.

What happens to my information?

It's used to file this claim and for nothing else.
