Questions

Who writes it, and why is it $99?

The draft is written with AI writing tools working from your materials and the funder's guidelines, not billed by the hour. That is why it costs $99 instead of the $1,500 to $5,000 commonly quoted for a foundation proposal. Every draft is then checked against the funder's requirements, and nothing is final until you approve it.

Do you guarantee we'll get the grant?

No, and no honest writer can: the funder decides. What you get is a complete application that answers every question within the funder's limits, delivered on time. The fee is the same whether or not you are funded, and it is never a percentage of the award.

What do you need from us?

The funder's application or guidelines, your answers to a short questionnaire, and your real numbers: who you serve, what the program costs, what it has achieved. We don't invent facts or figures. If something is missing, we flag it and ask.

Which grants does this cover?

Applications from nonprofits (or projects with a fiscal sponsor) to private, family, community and corporate foundations and local funders, up to 3,000 words of narrative. You choose the grant; we write the application. It doesn't cover federal or state government applications, research grants or grants to individuals, and it isn't a search for grants.

What if the funder has rules about AI?

Some funders ask applicants to disclose AI use, and a few don't allow it. We check your funder's guidelines for an AI policy before we start and tell you what we find. You review and approve everything, and the application goes out under your organization's name.

Our deadline is close. How fast is it?

The first draft arrives within 3 business days of receiving your materials, and each round of revisions within 2 business days. If your deadline is less than 5 business days away, say so before you start and we'll tell you whether it's realistic.

What happens to the documents we send?

They are used to write your application and for nothing else. We delete them 30 days after delivery, or sooner if you ask.
