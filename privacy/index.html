Privacy and contact

Short version: the services on this site are not open yet, the site takes no payments, and the only personal detail it stores is your email if you join a waitlist.

Who runs this

Flat Fee Desk is an independent, one-person business based in New York. It is not a law firm, an insurer, a medical provider or a government agency.

Contact: hello@flatfeedesk.com. A person reads it.

Where the services stand

The services described on this site are not open yet. The buttons lead to a waitlist, not a checkout. Nothing on this site charges you, and there is nowhere on it to upload a document.

What this site records

  • Your email address, only if you type it into a waitlist form. It is used to send your invite for that service and for nothing else.
  • Anonymous page events: that a page was opened, that a button was clicked, and which ad or site sent you here. These carry no name, no email and no account.

The site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Both kinds of record are stored in a private Google Sheet, so Google processes them on our behalf.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your email address.
  • We don't send marketing email. You get the invite and messages about the service you asked about.

Removing your details

Email hello@flatfeedesk.com from the address you signed up with and it will be deleted.
